# FuelCap product demonstration runbook

## Purpose

Show FuelCap as customers and business operators would use it. The presenter can explain separately that the environment uses representative sandbox data; the product interface should remain focused on customer value and business performance.

## Customer journey

1. Open the customer app and select **Create your profile**.
2. In **Wallet**, add `$250` using the saved payment method.
3. Open **Lock price**, compare one-station, one-brand and anywhere options, then choose the expected fuel volume.
4. Confirm protection and show the wallet funds becoming protected gallons.
5. Open **My tank**, select **Redeem**, and show the QR code to the retailer.
6. Select **Retailer confirms 10 gal**. Show the reduced tank balance, actual pump price, FuelCap-covered amount and Activity record.
7. Return Home to show nearby prices, savings and current protection availability.

## Business view

Open **Business Overview** in FuelCap Operations and explain:

1. Customer funds held, available, allocated and settling.
2. Protected volume and weighted customer protection price.
3. Current claims exposure and reserve coverage.
4. Fees, protection cost, insurance recovery, claims and operating contribution.
5. Reference purchase, customer protection and redemption prices.
6. Estimated claims, cover and reserve use under several price scenarios.

Use **Customer pricing controls** only to illustrate a market-price update or pausing new protection. Advanced operations and technical controls remain collapsed unless the audience asks how a business figure is governed or evidenced.

## Boundaries

The sandbox does not activate production providers or move real money. Avoid making unverified claims about certification, regulatory approval, insurance placement or live financial execution.
